## Meeting Notes

### Opening Remarks & Support Overview

* Tyler Wright welcomed participants and explained that SIG Support focuses on technical support issues related to the Akash Network codebase, Node, and Provider.
* The meeting follows a regular process of reviewing issues awaiting triage, discussing open support issues, and identifying recurring support themes from Discord and other community channels.
* Console-specific issues are maintained in their respective repositories, while the Support repository remains focused on core network issues.

### Support Repository & Issue Triage

* Scott Carruthers reviewed the Support repository and confirmed that only one issue was awaiting triage.
* The reported issue involved a deployment UI problem within Provider Console rather than the core Support repository.
* Scott and Tyler agreed that the issue should be closed in the Support repository and redirected to the Provider Console repository.
* Following that action, no additional Support issues remained awaiting triage.

### Community Technical Contributions

* Tyler encouraged technically experienced community members to use SIG Support and open GitHub issues as opportunities to contribute to Akash.
* Contributors can review the codebase, investigate open issues, and propose potential solutions to technical problems.
* Community members were also encouraged to participate in the Akash Website Working Group by testing network features and helping improve documentation.

### Home Node Beta Support

* Tyler highlighted the broader public beta rollout of Home Node during the previous weeks.
* A dedicated Home Node beta channel is available in Discord where participants can ask specific technical questions and interact with the Home Node team.
* No significant or unusual Home Node support problems were raised during the meeting.

### Community Support Updates

* Tyler invited Rodri R and other active community contributors to share recurring support issues observed across Discord, Reddit, Telegram, and other channels.
* No major support themes or unusual network problems were identified.
* Tyler recognized community contributors who regularly help users with deployments, Provider setup, Console templates, and general Akash onboarding.

### Support Process & Community Participation

* Community members were reminded that support issues can be raised asynchronously between monthly meetings.
* Contributors can post directly in the SIG Support channel or comment on existing GitHub issues for assistance from Core Team and community members.
* Technical community members looking for opportunities to contribute were encouraged to review open Support issues as a way to understand the Akash codebase and demonstrate their skills.

### Closing Remarks

* Tyler Wright thanked Scott Carruthers for leading the issue triage process and thanked community members who continue providing technical support across Akash channels.
* Participants were encouraged to direct new technical contributors toward SIG Support, open issues, and other community Working Groups.
* The meeting concluded with no additional support issues requiring discussion.
