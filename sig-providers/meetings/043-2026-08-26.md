## Meeting Notes

### Home Node Beta

* Tyler confirmed that **Akash Home Node is now in public beta**, following discussions in previous SIG Providers meetings.
* Home Node provides a simpler option for eligible GPU owners who do not want to operate a full standalone Akash provider.
* Users can install the Home Node ISO, add supported hardware, and begin making their resources available through Akash.
* Jacob confirmed that eligible participants can now join the public beta directly without additional sign-up requirements.

### Provider Development & Testing

* Tyler reiterated that Akash is looking for community members interested in testing provider improvements.
* New provider functionality is generally tested locally and in sandbox environments before being included in provider releases.
* Zeke and the Core team continue to coordinate provider development and testing.
* No new provider releases were reported since the previous SIG Providers meeting.

### Provider Communication

* Providers were encouraged to obtain the **Provider role in Discord** to receive relevant announcements in real time.
* The provider channels will be used to communicate upcoming releases, testing opportunities, and other provider-related developments.
* Provider discussions can continue asynchronously in Discord between monthly SIG meetings.

### Economics & Provider Programs

* Tyler noted that Community Pool fund returns and revenue sharing from certain provider programs would be discussed separately during SIG Economics.
* No additional provider-specific issues or questions were raised during the session.
