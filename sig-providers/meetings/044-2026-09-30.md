## Meeting Notes

### Provider v0.16.4 & ARM Support

- Tyler emphasized the importance of upgrading Akash providers to **version 0.16.4**, which introduces support for ARM architecture.
- The Core and Console teams have been working on ARM functionality, but broader provider adoption of the latest release is necessary to unlock its full capabilities.
- An upgrade announcement was previously shared in the Provider Announcements channel, with additional outreach from Akash Insiders and Vanguards.
- Providers who have not upgraded were strongly encouraged to do so to enable ARM workloads and related Console functionality.
- Community members were also asked to help communicate the importance of upgrading to other providers.

### Provider Auto-Upgrades & Infrastructure Management

- Tyler shared that the Core team has begun exploring **automatic provider upgrades** to simplify maintenance and improve adoption of future releases.
- The proposed functionality would reduce the operational burden of manually updating providers and help providers remain on supported versions.
- The Core team is also considering ways to abstract RPC node dependencies from providers, making live provider infrastructure easier to maintain and manage.
- These initiatives are still in the early planning stages, with implementation details and further announcements expected later.
- The broader objective is to improve provider reliability, reduce maintenance complexity, and simplify day-to-day provider operations.

### Community Proposals & Provider Feedback

- Tyler highlighted new discussions and proposals submitted through SIG Providers, including a proposal associated with **NATA** and a partnership proposal involving **Akash Gateway Agent on Elysium**.
- The Elysium-related proposal was described as a potential partnership opportunity involving a recently launched protocol, believed to be within the Cosmos ecosystem.
- Community members were encouraged to review the proposals and share questions, concerns, or suggestions through the SIG Providers channel.
- Tyler also acknowledged an Akash provider who has been publishing monthly updates about provider operations and infrastructure management.
- These operational reports provide useful insight into real-world provider experiences and help the Core team identify opportunities to improve provider tooling and workflows.
- Additional providers were encouraged to share similar experiences and operational feedback.

### Provider Audits & Outstanding Requests

- Tyler acknowledged delays affecting several outstanding provider audits.
- The Core team member responsible for coordinating audits had been on paternity leave, contributing to slower processing.
- Provider audits have also become more rigorous following previous incidents involving abuse of trial resources.
- The increased scrutiny is intended to reduce misuse and improve confidence in provider operations.
- Roger confirmed that several community providers had been asking about pending audits and that he had advised them to expect responses through Discord.
- Tyler thanked Roger for supporting affected providers and committed to bringing the outstanding audits to the Core team's attention.
- The team intends to prioritize pending audits and provide follow-up responses as quickly as possible, with Tyler targeting the following day or so.

### A100 GPU Demand & LLM Inference Optimization

- Tyler addressed community questions regarding demand for **NVIDIA A100 GPUs** on Akash.
- He shared that a team has been actively testing and optimizing A100 GPUs for inference workloads involving different large language models.
- The testing focuses on improving efficiency and determining whether A100 resources can deliver profitable inference performance.
- Tyler noted that A100 GPUs are attracting significant interest because of their pricing relative to other GPU options.
- However, the economic viability of A100 inference depends on achieving sufficient performance and utilization.
- The testing team was expected to share its findings with members of the Core team on October 1, 2026.
- Once reviewed, relevant results will be communicated to the broader Akash community, including SIG Providers.
- No final performance benchmarks or profitability conclusions were presented during the meeting.

### Closing Remarks

- Tyler reiterated that upgrading providers to **v0.16.4** remains a priority for enabling broader ARM support.
- The Core team will continue exploring auto-upgrades and improved infrastructure management tooling.
- Outstanding provider audits will receive additional attention, with updates communicated through Discord.
- Further information about A100 optimization and inference demand will be shared when testing results become available.
- Community members were encouraged to continue discussions asynchronously through the SIG Providers channel.
- Tyler thanked Scott, Roger, and community members following the meeting recording.
