## Meeting Notes

### Website & Documentation Updates

* Tyler led the meeting in Piyush’s absence and highlighted recent website and documentation changes.
* The Core team has been making CLI improvements, accompanied by updated documentation covering CLI installation and usage.
* Website development remains open source through the Akash Network GitHub organization, with community members encouraged to contribute.

### Bits Blog & AI Content

* Joe introduced **Bits**, a new frequently updated blog focused on AI models, GPU releases, benchmarks, AI deployments, and related industry developments.
* The blog is intended to connect broader AI and compute developments with ways those technologies can be deployed on Akash.
* The team is considering renaming the blog in the future and plans to maintain a high publishing frequency.

### GPU, Enterprise & Website Pages

* Community contributors are developing GPU-specific pages explaining the capabilities and advantages of GPUs available through the Akash marketplace.
* An enterprise page is also being developed to showcase Akash use cases, customer stories, and case studies.
* Dennis shared plans to consolidate several existing and upcoming pages under a unified **Use Cases** experience.
* Comparison pages are also planned to explain how Akash differs from neocloud and traditional cloud providers.
* A new case study PR was submitted for review, with Dennis agreeing to review its content and graphics while Piyush is unavailable.

### Community & Student Ambassador Content

* A significant batch of Student Ambassador content has been published under the website’s Community Contributions section.
* Rather than creating a separate Student Ambassador blog, Dennis recommended keeping contributions within one community content hub and using category filtering.
* The group discussed adding a link from the main Student Ambassador page directly to Student Ambassador contributions.
* Dennis agreed to explore the best placement for this connection and noted that additional broader website changes are still under development.

### Contributions & Next Steps

* Dmitry joined the session and asked how he could contribute to Akash.
* Tyler directed him to the open-source website repository, where contributors can review issues, PRs, architecture, documentation, and content opportunities.
* Joe offered to help new contributors identify suitable tasks, bounties, and opportunities based on their skills.
* Additional branding, enterprise, and website improvements are expected to be presented during future Working Group sessions.
