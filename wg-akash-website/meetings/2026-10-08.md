## Meeting Notes

### Website Design & UI Consistency

- Denis highlighted ongoing efforts to unify the Akash website and other digital products under a consistent UI component library.
- The design team has been gradually updating individual website sections to align with shared visual components, typography, and interaction patterns.
- A significant typography update replaced **Inter with Geist** across the website.
- The changes are intended to create a more consistent visual experience across Akash Console, the website, and other digital touchpoints.

### Blog Listing & Article Page Improvements

- The blog listing page received a redesigned interface featuring updated content tiles and improved navigation.
- New filtering options allow users to browse posts by year and topic, with additional sorting functionality.
- An alternative text-focused view was introduced for users who prefer browsing article headlines, dates, and authors without images.
- A bug that displayed a hardcoded five-minute reading time across all posts was fixed. Articles now display their respective reading times.
- The blog detail page received typography refinements, including updated font sizes and weights.
- Breadcrumb navigation was added to improve movement between the blog index, categories, and individual articles.
- Denis also replaced a Console promotional banner with an **AkashML banner** as an experiment to evaluate whether it generates stronger click-through engagement.
- The experiment is intended to inform future promotional placements based on user behavior.

### Usage Pricing & Provider Earnings Calculators

- The website pricing page received improvements to both the usage pricing calculator and Provider Earnings Calculator.
- Popular deployment templates were added to help users quickly estimate monthly compute costs.
- Users can select predefined workloads, such as OpenClaw, to populate relevant pricing estimates.
- The pricing calculator also supports GPU model selection and adjustable GPU quantities.
- At the time of the meeting, comparisons with hyperscalers such as Google and Microsoft were available, but GPU-specific comparative pricing was not yet fully supported.
- Denis indicated that additional GPU pricing comparisons would be introduced later.
- The **Provider Earnings Calculator** now allows prospective providers to select different GPU models, configure compute resources, adjust pricing, and experiment with utilization assumptions.
- Based on these inputs, the calculator produces estimated earnings, helping providers evaluate potential returns from offering compute resources on Akash.

### Compute Providers Page & Homepage Updates

- Denis demonstrated the redesigned **Compute Providers** page, which incorporates visual components and functionality adapted from Akash Console.
- The page features an interactive globe displaying provider locations across the network.
- Users can select individual provider locations to view summaries of available resources and provider details.
- The interface allows users to navigate directly toward deployment or open a more detailed provider profile.
- Expanded provider pages provide additional information about available resources, offerings, and pricing.
- A complete provider listing is also available below the interactive globe.
- The same globe-based provider experience has been incorporated into the lower section of the homepage.
- A bug affecting interaction with provider nodes on the globe was also fixed.

### Provider Playbooks & Home Node Navigation

- Denis confirmed that provider-related calls to action have been updated following previous Working Group discussions.
- Users interested in contributing compute resources are now directed toward either **Home Node** or **Provider Playbooks**, depending on their intended setup.
- The Provider Playbooks option links directly to the documentation for automated provider setup.
- Outdated links directing users to Provider Console for provider creation have been replaced.
- These updates reinforce the distinction between simplified Home Node participation and the full provider setup process using Provider Playbooks.

### AI Documentation & Search Discoverability

- Piyush shared a high-level overview of recent documentation and technical website improvements.
- The agent-focused **Get Started** documentation was reworked around deploying workloads through Akash Console.
- The Markdown-based guidance works alongside the website's `llms.txt` file to help AI agents and language models understand how to deploy applications on Akash.
- Article FAQ structured data and breadcrumb structured data were added to improve how search engines interpret website content.
- Per-page sitemap modification dates were corrected to provide more accurate information about content updates.
- These changes support both traditional search discoverability and AI-assisted access to Akash documentation.

### Campaign Landing Page & Lead Generation

- Piyush highlighted a dedicated landing page created for Akash's campaign on the **Big Technology Podcast**.
- The campaign includes advertising placements on the podcast, with a corresponding landing page containing relevant information for interested listeners.
- The page is intended to attract potential users and capture leads generated through the campaign.
- The team will observe whether the campaign generates additional traffic and interest in Akash.

### GPU Pages & Contact Form Improvements

- The GPUs On-Demand page was simplified, and its form received visual improvements.
- The Get in Touch page was also streamlined following feedback from an earlier Working Group meeting.
- Unnecessary marketing sections and supporting value propositions were removed from the bottom of the page.
- The updated contact experience focuses on a headline, brief explanatory text, and the submission form.
- The objective is to reduce distractions for visitors who have already demonstrated an intention to contact Akash.

### Website Bug Fixes & Technical Maintenance

- Several smaller fixes were completed across the website.
- Mobile GPU pricing rows were improved to display more effectively on smaller screens.
- The provider explorer globe interaction bug was resolved.
- A homepage issue affecting the **Efficiency Gap** pricing display was fixed.
- Previously, navigating away from the homepage and returning could cause Efficiency Gap figures to disappear or fail to populate.
- The figures now render correctly on repeat visits.
- Directly imported dependencies were also declared as part of the technical maintenance updates.
- These fixes improve website reliability, navigation, and consistency across devices.

### Closing Remarks

- Denis thanked Piyush for sharing the consolidated overview of recent website and documentation changes.
- The group confirmed that the major updates from the previous two weeks had been covered.
- Community members were encouraged to report bugs, suggest improvements, and participate in discussions through the Akash Website Working Group Discord channel.
- The next regular meeting is expected in two weeks.
