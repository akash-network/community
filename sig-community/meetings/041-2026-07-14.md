## Meeting Notes

### Opening Remarks & Community Updates

* Joe Deng welcomed attendees and opened the monthly Community SIG meeting with updates on current community initiatives.
* Participants held informal discussions around AI models, infrastructure providers, startup development, and GPU deployments before the official presentation.
* John McCoy introduced the Digital Frontier provider team, sharing that their European infrastructure continues to expand and that all of their providers operate together as a unified deployment mesh.
* Joe Deng expressed interest in learning more about the provider network and encouraged continued collaboration with infrastructure partners.

### Linear Project Management

* Joe Deng announced that all Akash Club projects, bounties, grants, and contributor initiatives are now managed through **Linear**.
* Contributors can request access to the workspace through Discord to follow project progress, discover new opportunities, and collaborate more effectively.
* New community ideas will continue to be reviewed before becoming official tracked projects within the Linear workspace.
* The goal is to centralize community operations and simplify project coordination as contributor activity continues to grow.

### Community Projects

* Joe Deng introduced the **Akash AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** initiative, which focuses on improving how AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok discover and recommend Akash through AI-optimized educational content.
* The initiative includes creating technical blogs, infrastructure articles, GPU benchmarks, and educational resources designed specifically for modern AI search experiences.
* The **Short-form Content Initiative** continues to expand beyond the Student Ambassador Program, encouraging contributors to create authentic educational videos that introduce Akash to developers and first-time users.
* Joe Deng emphasized producing human-created content that explains practical Akash use cases rather than highly technical or crypto-focused material.
* Updates were shared on the **Supply and Demand Initiative**, where contributors are helping identify new GPU providers, data centers, AI startups, and enterprise customers to strengthen both sides of the Akash marketplace.
* Community members were encouraged to continue proposing independent project ideas that can evolve into official bounty opportunities.
* Joe Deng explained that future deployment templates will now include supporting quick-start guides, educational documentation, promotional articles, and video walkthroughs to improve adoption.
* The OpenProject deployment template is currently being expanded with documentation and educational content, while work also continues on the Paperclip Agent Orchestration template.

### Community Applications

* Joe Deng shared that **Akash Agents** will remain a bring-your-own-key platform, allowing users to deploy AI agents using their own Akash Console and Akash ML API credentials.
* Future improvements will focus on practical deployment guides and real-world examples to make AI agents more accessible to new users.
* Sandeep Narahari's AI reminder agent was highlighted as a successful community project that helps monitor medication and meal reminders for his father, demonstrating meaningful real-world applications of Akash-hosted AI.
* Joe Deng announced that development of **Akash Scribe** has been completed but the project is temporarily paused until Akash ML introduces speech-to-text model support.
* Once speech models become available, Akash Scribe will launch as a pay-as-you-go transcription application powered entirely by Akash infrastructure.

### Upcoming Events & Community Engagement

* Joe Deng announced the next **Akash Club Fireside Chat**, featuring Jigar, one of the lead engineers behind Akash Home Node.
* The discussion will focus on simplified GPU provider onboarding, Home Node development, and future roadmap updates.
* Community members unable to attend live were encouraged to register in advance to receive the recording.
* Joe Deng reminded attendees about **Akash Accelerate**, encouraging community members to participate either in person or virtually as preparations continue.
* A preview of the upcoming **Akash Virtual Community Space**, being developed with Passage, showcased plans for virtual meetups, networking, breakout rooms, and future live community events hosted on Akash infrastructure.
* Joe Deng shared his vision of creating a permanent virtual home for Akash Club where community members can gather regardless of their physical location.

### GitHub Discussions & Grant Proposals

* Joe Deng encouraged contributors to actively participate in GitHub Discussions, emphasizing that community feedback directly influences ecosystem development and grant decisions.
* A grant proposal from student ambassador **Zestflow** was presented, focusing on simplifying GPU deployments for scientific research and university workloads.
* The **Jini Launchpad** proposal was introduced, requesting AKT deployment credits for an AI-powered infrastructure deployment platform.
* Community members were encouraged to review an invitation for Akash to participate in an upcoming AI & Crypto Expo through sponsorship, hackathons, and compute credit programs.
* A benchmarking proposal requesting AKT credits to evaluate Akash's low-latency performance for cryptocurrency trading workloads was also presented for community feedback.

### Community Questions & Closing Remarks

* Sandeep Narahari asked whether future deployment templates would receive promotional support after publication.
* Joe Deng confirmed that completed templates will be promoted through the Akash Club X account, educational articles, quick-start guides, video tutorials, and the official community website.
* Joe Deng encouraged contributors to discuss new template ideas before beginning development to ensure they align with community priorities and bounty opportunities.
* Looking ahead, Joe Deng announced that future Community SIG meetings will likely begin with GitHub Discussions to improve participation and visibility.
* The meeting concluded with thanks to attendees and an invitation to continue contributing through Discord, GitHub Discussions, and Akash Club initiatives.
