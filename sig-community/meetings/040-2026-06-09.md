### Akash Agents Platform

* Joe Deng provided an update on **Akash Agents**, the community platform for deploying AI agents on Akash Network.
* Explained that the platform enables users to quickly deploy AI agents with different capabilities depending on their needs.
* Shared that current development is focused primarily on:
  * OpenClaw
  * Hermes
* Announced that Rodri R is integrating LiveKit to introduce voice-agent capabilities.
* Explained that the long-term vision is to build a comprehensive collection of AI agents that users can launch whenever needed.
* Announced that the platform is currently available through the existing community deployment portal.
* Shared plans to migrate the platform onto a new Akash Club domain as part of a redesigned community experience.
* Explained that the current deployment workflow requires users to provide:
  * Akash Console API Keys
  * AkashML API Keys
* Announced that future releases will remove this requirement by introducing a fully managed deployment service.
* Explained that the backend infrastructure will handle authentication and deployment automatically.
* Stated that the new managed service is designed to simplify AI deployment for less technical users.
* Announced that a beta program will launch soon.
* Invited community members to register through Discord or Telegram.
* Shared that Akash will pay for participants' first deployed agent during the beta.
* Explained that after beta, pricing plans will be introduced to cover compute costs.
* Mentioned that future plans include bundled Akash credit packages for users.

### Akash Scribe

* Joe Deng introduced **Akash Scribe**, a community-developed AI dictation application.
* Explained that Akash Scribe allows users to dictate speech directly into any application by inserting transcribed text wherever the user's cursor is located.
* Described speech-to-text as one of the most practical day-to-day AI applications.
* Announced that Akash Scribe is also approaching beta release.
* Shared that beta signups are already available through the Scribe registration page.
* Invited additional testers to participate before launch.
* Explained that beta testers will help validate:
  * Application stability
  * User experience
  * Performance
  * Latency
* Recognized Rodri R for actively maintaining the application throughout development.
* Explained that Akash Scribe will continue receiving updates throughout the beta period.
* Stated that once launched, Akash Scribe will remain a community-maintained project intended to demonstrate practical AI workloads on Akash Network.

### Current GitHub Discussions

#### Umee IBC Recovery Proposal

* Joe Deng highlighted the proposal to recover approximately 40,000 AKT locked following the Umee shutdown.
* Explained that the proposal would reopen the IBC pathway long enough for affected users to recover their assets.
* Described the proposal as a straightforward community benefit.

#### SoverGrid Proposal

* Joe Deng discussed the SoverGrid proposal requesting Community Pool funding.
* Explained that SoverGrid aims to provide a decentralized AWS-style command line deployment experience for Akash.
* Noted that the proposal also includes a long-term revenue-sharing model.
* Shared his personal opinion that combining Community Pool funding with ongoing revenue sharing could represent double compensation.
* Encouraged community members to carefully evaluate the proposal and contribute their own perspectives before governance voting.

#### Revenue Sharing Proposal

* Joe Deng reviewed the **[Revenue Sharing Proposal](https://github.com/orgs/akash-network/discussions/1408)**.
* Explained that the proposal clarifies the previously undefined "management fee" introduced under PIP 3.5.
* Discussed differences between:
  * Spot deployments.
  * Reserved infrastructure deployments.
* Explained that spot deployments require relatively little operational overhead.
* Noted that reservation contracts require:
  * Sales efforts.
  * Customer management.
  * Contract administration.
  * SLA enforcement.
  * Provider coordination.
* Explained that the proposal defines:
  * 10% operational share for spot deployments.
  * 30% operational share for reserved deployments.
* Encouraged community members to provide feedback before the proposal advances to governance voting.

### Community Q&A

#### Akash Scribe Language Support

* Alejandro asked whether Akash Scribe would support languages beyond English.
* Joe Deng explained that language support ultimately depends on the speech-to-text models hosted through AkashML.
* Rodri R confirmed that the current beta model supports multiple languages.
* Joe Deng noted that multilingual support is expected during beta but future production capabilities depend on which models AkashML ultimately hosts.
* Rodri R expressed optimism that supported speech models would eventually include Scandinavian languages.
* Joe Deng explained that AkashML will likely prioritize models with broad adoption in order to maximize GPU utilization.

#### Compute Economics

* Joe Deng explained the economic rationale behind using AkashML rather than self-hosted infrastructure.
* Estimated that independently hosting speech-to-text models could cost approximately $500 per month.
* Explained that community applications are intentionally priced near compute cost rather than maximizing profit.
* Shared that projected subscription pricing may fall between approximately $6 and $8 per month.
* Emphasized that the objective is to:
  * Cover compute expenses.
  * Increase demand on Akash Network.
  * Demonstrate practical AI applications.
* Noted that latency testing during beta will provide valuable feedback for improving AkashML.
* Explained that successful community applications ultimately help strengthen the broader Akash ecosystem by generating real production workloads.
