## Meeting Notes

### Akash AEO & The Bid

* The Akash **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** initiative remains an active long-term community project focused on increasing how frequently Akash appears in answers generated by AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, and Grok.
* The objective is for Akash to surface naturally when users ask questions such as where to access GPUs or deploy AI workloads, combining AEO with traditional SEO efforts.
* The previously launched **Bits** blog has been renamed **The Bid**, creating a stronger connection to Akash Network and its marketplace bidding model.
* The Bid is primarily intended to publish information that can be discovered, indexed, and referenced by AI answer engines, although the content remains publicly readable.
* Sandeep is currently the core contributor working on the initiative alongside the community team.
* Work is underway to establish measurable KPIs that can determine whether publishing and promoting content through The Bid is improving Akash’s visibility in AI-generated results.
* Community members interested in writing additional content were encouraged to join the initiative so content production does not depend on a single contributor.

### Student Ambassador Cohort 3

* The third cohort of the **Akash Student Ambassador Program** is beginning onboarding, with the new cohort larger than previous groups.
* Student ambassadors will continue supporting Akash through campus events, hackathons, educational activities, and community engagement.
* Cohort 3 will expand the program by having ambassadors conduct workshops for the broader Akash community.
* Workshops are expected to cover practical topics such as AI usage, tools, research, and other educational subjects beyond simply demonstrating how to deploy applications on Akash.
* Akash Club will provide a platform for ambassadors to research topics and present their findings to community members.
* The program is also forming an **Akash hackathon team** that will participate in AI-focused hackathons and build projects using Akash as the underlying infrastructure.
* The team is expected to have dedicated jerseys and participate in multiple hackathons as a recognizable Akash community team.
* George asked whether the Student Ambassador Program is limited to US universities or open to campuses internationally.
* The program currently focuses heavily on universities with strong AI programs in the US, although international ambassadors are also represented.
* George was encouraged to connect with Shelby, who manages the Student Ambassador Program, for further information about opportunities involving universities outside the US.

### Provider Sourcing Program

* A new **Provider Sourcing Program** has launched to incentivize community members to help Akash identify and onboard additional compute providers.
* The initiative reflects a shift in marketplace priorities: while previous efforts focused heavily on generating demand, the network now has growing demand and needs additional supply.
* Community members can participate by identifying data centers, infrastructure operators, GPU suppliers, and other organizations that directly control compute resources.
* A **$50 bounty** is available for qualifying provider introductions.
* Providers should generally have a minimum of approximately 10 nodes available and should directly control their compute rather than simply operating another marketplace.
* The program is primarily looking for direct sources of compute, although useful contacts and introductions are still welcomed.
* Contributors receive credit for their introductions so successful relationships can be traced back to the person who sourced the opportunity.
* If an introduction ultimately results in a commercial agreement, such as a one-year or two-year deal between a marketplace user and the introduced provider, an additional **$1,000 bounty** may be awarded.
* Community members interested in participating were encouraged to make contact with the community team first so they can receive guidance on qualification and outreach.

### Paperclip & Community Bounty Improvements

* The community is finalizing the **Paperclip** deployment, an agent orchestration framework that can now be hosted on Akash.
* Paperclip is being used as the first implementation of a more comprehensive process for community deployment bounties.
* Previously, completing a deployment bounty could primarily mean creating a Console template; the new process is designed to provide much more support around each template.
* Paperclip will include the Console template, a deployment guide explaining how to launch it, and a use-case guide explaining why users should consider deploying it.
* The project will also include a video deployment guide and a separate use-case video.
* Completed materials will be published through the **Community Contributions** section of the Akash website.
* The intention is to make future Console templates easier to understand, deploy, and adopt instead of simply adding templates without explaining their practical value.
* Omar, who also manages the Akashians X presence, is supporting video editing and presentation quality for community contributions.
* The team is experimenting with AI voiceovers to create more consistent and polished educational videos regardless of contributors’ accents, speaking styles, or voiceover experience.
* The first video using this workflow has been completed, with the accompanying use-case guide and video expected to follow.
* Future community template bounties are expected to adopt this broader template-plus-documentation-plus-video approach.

### Buzz Community Platform & AI Agents

* Work continues on deploying **Buzz**, a collaborative chat platform that can integrate AI agents alongside human community members.
* Rodrigo is currently leading the initiative and working through technical requirements beyond the basic Console deployment.
* A deployable Buzz template is already available on Console, but additional testing and documentation are required to make the application easier for other users to operate.
* Deployment requires additional configuration, including connecting an appropriate domain and configuring HTTPS.
* Akash Club plans to host its own Buzz instance and use it internally for community projects, bounties, and longer-term contributor collaboration.
* The community intends to use the product directly before finalizing its use-case documentation, allowing the eventual guide to reflect real operational experience.
* A community instance has already been deployed, with work underway to connect it to a proper HTTPS domain.
* Contributors working on bounties and longer-term projects will eventually be invited into the platform for testing.
* The community also plans to experiment with AI agents inside Buzz to determine how agents can assist with collaborative community work.
* Sandeep’s existing `agents.akash.network` work was highlighted as infrastructure that allows agents to be hosted on Akash and powered by Akash ML.
* The broader experiment will involve bringing multiple agents together within collaborative channels and evaluating how they can contribute to community workflows.

### GitHub Grant Requests & Discussions

* Community members were strongly encouraged to participate in active **GitHub Discussions**, particularly when proposals involve Community Pool funding or important product features.
* **AON Aegis** was highlighted as a proposal for relay infrastructure intended to obscure provider IP addresses and potentially provide additional protection against attacks such as denial-of-service attempts.
* **Shoestring** proposes simplifying Akash deployments by wrapping the deployment process into a more streamlined command that can help select suitable and cost-effective providers.
* The Shoestring proposal is requesting approximately **$41,000** and has already generated active discussion, including testing and feedback from an existing Akash provider.
* **Digital Frontier** has proposed developing shared Console account functionality that would allow organizations to provide team members access to deployments and resources under a shared funding structure.
* Shared Console functionality is already considered useful for Akash’s roadmap and could support organizations where multiple developers need access to a common account.
* Another grant request proposes training an **open-source Mixture-of-Experts LLM** designed for local deployment, with approximately **$1,000 in credits** requested for training.
* **Pantheon GPU** is seeking collaboration rather than direct funding to introduce more comprehensive GPU health checks into the Akash ecosystem.
* Current auditing verifies whether a provider actually possesses the GPU model it claims to offer, while Pantheon’s proposed approach would additionally evaluate the condition and operational health of that GPU.
* The proposed health checks could help identify issues such as degraded hardware or GPUs that are not operating as expected.
* Pantheon’s longer-term goal is to potentially contribute these health checks to the provider auditing process and improve the reliability of compute available through the marketplace.
