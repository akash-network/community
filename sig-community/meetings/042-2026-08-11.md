## Meeting Notes

### Opening Remarks & Akash Club Updates

* Joe Deng welcomed participants to the monthly SIG Community meeting and provided updates on ongoing Akash Club projects.
* Community projects are now managed through Linear, and contributors interested in participating can request access through Joe Deng.
* The Community Team continues to create new projects based on ecosystem needs and ideas submitted by contributors.

### Akash Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

* Akash AEO is a major community initiative focused on improving how AI assistants and LLMs discover, understand, and recommend Akash Network.
* The team audited Akash documentation to ensure pages are crawlable and accessible to LLMs, with Rodri R and Sandeep Narahari contributing to the initiative.
* The objective is for AI assistants to surface Akash when users ask about hosting new models, GPU infrastructure, enterprise compute, and related use cases.
* New AEO-focused pages are being developed around enterprise adoption, GPUs, case studies, and other Akash capabilities.

### Akash Bits & Documentation

* Akash Bits was introduced as a new content initiative connecting current AI and technology trends back to relevant Akash use cases.
* Initial articles cover topics such as AI data ownership, open-source models, Confidential Compute, and comparisons between emerging AI models.
* Community members are invited to contribute articles through an AI-assisted workflow that includes human fact-checking before publication.
* Rodri R submitted documentation improvements designed to make Akash documentation easier to export in Markdown and use with AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT.

### Community Content & Supply Search

* The short-form content initiative remains open for community contributors, with content being repurposed for platforms such as X and Instagram.
* Joe Deng recognized Amar for helping convert interviews and other long-form material into short-form community content.
* Demand-search activities have been reduced while Akash addresses its current supply crunch.
* Sandeep Narahari is leading the supply-search initiative by identifying providers, data centers, and compute owners that could bring additional infrastructure onto Akash.
* The supply initiative is expected to support future marketplace expansion by building relationships with potential infrastructure providers.

### Community Contributions & Templates

* Community members continue to be encouraged to bring new project and deployment ideas to Akash Club.
* Paperclip, an agent-oriented platform, has been added as an Akash Console deployment template.
* New template contributions will now require more than simply publishing a deployable template.
* Contributors will also be expected to provide a deployment guide and a practical use case demonstrating why users should deploy the application.
* The objective is to prioritize useful templates that generate actual deployments rather than building a large collection of unused templates.

### Agentic Community Workflows

* Rodri R is working on Buzz, described as a self-hosted communication environment where users and AI agents can interact in the same workspace.
* The Community Team plans to explore combining Buzz with the existing Akash Agents platform developed with contributions from Sandeep Narahari.
* The longer-term goal is to use Linear for project management and an agent-powered communication layer for community collaboration.
* Joe Deng emphasized that the Akash community should actively use agentic infrastructure itself while encouraging others to deploy similar technology on Akash.

### Akash Club Podcast

* Joe Deng provided an update on the monthly Akash Club podcast and fireside chats.
* Recent discussions included conversations with Jigar about Home Node and Greg Osuri about community questions and concerns.
* Joe Deng plans to hold a broader community discussion with Greg approximately every six months to provide greater transparency around ecosystem developments.
* An upcoming episode is planned with Joseph Chalabi to discuss the upgraded Akash CLI, new functionality, and related engineering projects.

### Akash Accelerate

* Joe Deng announced that Akash Accelerate 2026 will no longer take place as previously planned.
* The event is expected to be moved to 2027, potentially during the first half of the year.
* The decision allows the team to prioritize current network needs, particularly addressing the supply crunch, instead of allocating significant resources toward organizing a major conference.
* Additional information regarding a future Akash Accelerate event will be shared once plans are finalized.

### GitHub Discussions & Governance

* Joe Deng encouraged community members to participate more actively in GitHub Discussions and publicly share their views on grants, funding requests, and governance matters.
* Several Core Engineering and support-service funding proposals were highlighted, including proposals covering work already completed for Akash.
* Current grant discussions include an open-source Mixture-of-Experts model deployment project and a telemetry layer intended to provide additional information about deployment containers.
* A significant institutional custody integration request was also highlighted, with Joe encouraging the community to carefully review the proposed cost and benefits.
* Digital Frontier submitted a proposal related to improving enterprise account invoicing, including support for multiple users being invoiced from the same account.
* Community members were encouraged to review proposals directly on GitHub and direct deeper economic and funding discussions toward SIG Economics.

### Closing Remarks

* Joe Deng encouraged community members to contribute ideas, content, provider leads, deployment templates, and feedback on governance proposals.
* Rodri R and Sandeep Narahari confirmed there were no additional questions or topics requiring discussion.
* The meeting concluded after approximately forty minutes.
