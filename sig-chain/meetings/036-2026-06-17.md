## Meeting Notes

### Opening Remarks

* Tyler Wright welcomed participants and noted that Artur and Scott were unavailable, with Zeke representing the Core Engineering team.
* The meeting focused primarily on reviewing the successful Mainnet 18 network upgrade.

### Akash Mainnet 18 Upgrade

* Mainnet 18 was successfully completed on June 11, 2026, with the upgrade taking less than two minutes.
* Tyler thanked validators for preparing and supporting the network upgrade.
* The upgrade introduced Oracle V2 improvements for Burn Mint Equilibrium (BME), Resource Reclamation, and a fix for a provider-side Market Order Close issue.

### Oracle V2 & Resource Reclamation

* Oracle V2 improves pricing stability and reduces incidents that previously required manual monitoring.
* Resource Reclamation is now available following both the network and provider releases.
* Documentation has been published for providers and deployers.
* The feature is expected to support enterprise customers that occasionally need to reclaim infrastructure while maintaining production workloads.

### Provider Bug Fix

* The upgrade resolved an issue where some deployments could become stuck during provider operations due to orphaned escrow or bid-related states.
* Although the issue was isolated, it occurred frequently enough to require a network upgrade.

### Off-Chain Acceptance (Marketplace V2)

* The Core Engineering team is now focusing on the next major initiative previously known as Marketplace V2, now called **Off-Chain Acceptance**.
* The feature will introduce support for Virtual Machines (VMs) and Bare Metal deployments on Akash.
* Initial implementation and testing have been successful, with development continuing alongside a new AEP.
* A future network upgrade will be required once implementation is complete.

### Validator & Community Updates

* Validators were encouraged to join the Validator Discord channel to receive upgrade notifications and technical discussions.
* Community members were reminded about upcoming SIG Economics, SIG Providers, and Website Working Group meetings.
* Tyler encouraged contributors to participate in Discord roles and community working groups.

### Closing Remarks

* Tyler Wright thanked participants and encouraged continued involvement across Akash community meetings.
