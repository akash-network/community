## Meeting Notes

### BME Resilience

* The Core Team has been working to improve the resilience of the Burn Mint Equilibrium (BME) implementation following reliability issues with the existing AKT price source.
* The work requires new smart contracts and coordinated governance actions from Akash validators.
* Validators were briefed through email and the Validator Discord channel and asked to support an expedited upgrade process.

### Pyth Price Feed Migration

* Akash is migrating away from the existing Wormhole guardian signature mechanism used by Pyth price feeds.
* The migration is required because Pyth is changing its price verification architecture after intermittent guardian issues affected price feeds across multiple networks.
* The previous Pyth architecture was expected to be discontinued around August 18, making the migration important for maintaining reliable AKT pricing.

### Smart Contract Upgrade

* The migration will occur in multiple phases, beginning with governance proposals to upload new Pyth Pro and Pyth 3A contracts.
* Separate proposals are required because of Akash's transaction-size limits.
* After approval, additional proposals will instantiate the contracts and configure the new signature verification architecture.
* New Hermes relayers will then forward prices from the updated endpoints.
* The old contracts will be retired after the Core Team confirms the new infrastructure is operating correctly.

### Governance & Validator Coordination

* The contract proposals will use expedited governance.
* Validators were asked to vote within the first 24 hours of each proposal becoming available so the migration can be completed quickly.
* Additional parameter-change proposals were expected to follow shortly.
* The Validator Discord channel will serve as the primary coordination point for questions and updates during the migration.

### Closing Remarks
