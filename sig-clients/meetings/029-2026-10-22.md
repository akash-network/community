## Meeting Notes

### Akash Console Redesign & User Experience

- Josh shared updates on recent Akash Console improvements, including the completion of an end-to-end redesign approximately two weeks before the meeting.
- The redesign introduces a unified design language and updated interface patterns across Console.
- The deployment configuration page was also updated following extensive user research and testing.
- Early analytics indicate improvements in deployment fulfillment and the ability of users to receive provider bids after configuring their SDL files.
- The redesigned Console received positive feedback during a recent hackathon in San Francisco, particularly regarding its improved ease of use.
- Josh emphasized that the team will continue monitoring product metrics and user feedback to identify further improvements.

### Organizations & Collaborative Deployment Management

- Josh announced the launch of the **Organizations** feature in Akash Console.
- Organizations allow multiple users to share spaces and collaborate on deployment management.
- The feature is intended to simplify coordination for teams managing workloads on Akash.
- A **Recent Activity** feature was also introduced, allowing users to see actions performed within shared spaces.
- These improvements provide greater visibility into team activities and make collaborative deployment management more efficient.
- Tyler acknowledged feedback from the Digital Frontier team, which contributed to discussions around the Organizations feature.

### Keyboard Navigation & Shortcuts

- Console now supports keyboard navigation and shortcuts to improve efficiency.
- A search bar at the top of Console opens a modal displaying available keyboard commands and shortcuts.
- Users can access frequently used functions and navigate the interface more quickly.
- The feature is particularly useful for experienced users who prefer keyboard-driven workflows.

### Upcoming Projects Feature

- Josh previewed **Projects**, an upcoming Console feature designed to complement Organizations.
- Projects will allow users to group related deployments for easier organization and management.
- The feature is intended to support more structured workflows for teams managing multiple deployments.
- Future improvements may include additional capabilities for managing and updating deployments within projects.
- Josh indicated that Projects could launch as early as the following day or the next week, although no confirmed release date was provided.

### Console Air & Open-Source Clients

- Tyler highlighted **Console Air**, a wallet-based version of Akash Console that can be self-hosted.
- Console Air is available at `air.akash.network`, with its source code hosted within the Akash Network GitHub organization.
- Community members can deploy and manage their own Console Air instances.
- Tyler encouraged developers building clients and applications on Akash to participate in SIG Clients discussions.
- The main Akash Console is also open source, with its repository available for reviewing changes, reporting issues, and contributing improvements.
- Console and CLI-related questions can be raised through their respective Discord channels.

### Akash ML & GPU Infrastructure

- Tyler highlighted ongoing developments around **Akash ML**, an inference offering developed by Overclock Labs and powered by Akash Network providers.
- Akash ML is available through `akashml.com` and is also distributed through OpenRouter and other inference platforms.
- The product uses GPU resources supplied through the Akash Network.
- Tyler noted continuing demand for certain GPU types and encouraged providers with available GPU capacity to consider joining the network.
- Akash ML provides another example of an application leveraging decentralized compute infrastructure.

### Akash ML Trials & Upcoming UX Improvements

- Tyler explained that Akash ML trial offerings had been modified following incidents of trial abuse.
- The changes were intentional and designed to address misuse of the service.
- Additional Akash ML onboarding and user experience improvements are planned over the coming weeks.
- Tyler encouraged users to share feedback through the dedicated Akash ML Discord channel.
- Unlike Akash Console, Akash ML is a **closed-source product**, but the product team continues to monitor community feedback and suggestions.
- No detailed release specifications or confirmed launch dates were provided for the upcoming changes.

### Digital Frontier & Community Client Development

- Tyler acknowledged the **Digital Frontier** team for its contributions and feedback regarding collaborative deployment management.
- Digital Frontier is a European-based team building on top of the Akash Network, with a focus on European users and providers.
- Tyler highlighted Digital Frontier as another option within the Akash client ecosystem alongside Console and Console Air.
- Community members interested in developing clients or improving existing products were encouraged to engage with other builders and participate in ongoing discussions.

### Community Feedback & Product Roadmap

- Tyler encouraged users to report bugs, suggest improvements, and share questions through the SIG Clients, Akash Console, CLI, and Akash ML Discord channels.
- Community members interested in alpha and beta testing were encouraged to express their interest to the product team.
- The Akash Console GitHub repository remains available for issue tracking and open-source contributions.
- Additional Console roadmap updates are expected, allowing contributors to follow upcoming development priorities.
- Tyler confirmed that meeting recordings, notes, and transcripts would be shared for those unable to attend.

### Closing Remarks

- Tyler thanked Josh for presenting the recent Console improvements and Julius for attending the meeting.
- He recognized the contributions of the product, engineering, and design teams.
- Tyler also thanked George from the Akash community for his assistance with meeting documentation.
- Community members were encouraged to continue discussions asynchronously between meetings.
- The meeting concluded with a reminder that SIG Clients meets every two months.
