## Meeting Notes

### BME & Economics Updates

* Tyler reported that the previously discussed Burn-Mint Equilibrium (BME) changes have been implemented and appear to be functioning as designed.
* The Core Engineering team continues exploring additional backup and resilience mechanisms for BME.
* PIT smart contracts are live on the Akash Network, with further resilience discussions expected in future SIG Economics and SIG Chain meetings.

### Community Pool & Funding

* Isaac reported limited Community Pool proposal activity during the month.
* AEP funding requests are moving to a **quarterly cadence** rather than being submitted as individual development efforts are completed.
* With the current quarter ending in September, the next round of funding proposals is expected to go on-chain around October.
* The change is intended to better align funding with faster and more focused development cycles.

### PIP Strategy & Community Pool Returns

* The team is strategically moving away from relying on the PIP structure to fund providers.
* Isaac expects significantly fewer PIP funding requests, while more GPUs are expected to join the network without PIP funding.
* Approximately **888,156 AKT** was returned to the Community Pool during the previous 30 days.
* These returns included revenue sharing from PIP providers and unused funds from liquidation/volatility buffers.
* Approximately **560,000 AKT** of the returned amount came from unused volatility buffer funds.
* Isaac expects additional funds to be returned as the network scales and market conditions improve.

### Validator Program

* Tyler noted that some validators have recently left the validator program while new participants have joined.
* The Akash Foundation's historical delegation framework is currently **on hold** while broader Foundation matters are being addressed.
* Tyler indicated that the pause is expected to continue for at least the next couple of months.
* New validators may still participate in Community Pool opportunities, community bounties, and other ecosystem programs while alternative compensation mechanisms are considered.

### Closing Remarks

* No additional AKT, BME, or economics-related questions were raised during the session.
* Community members were encouraged to submit topics and questions through the SIG Economics channel for future meetings.
* Further BME resilience, Community Pool, and network economics updates will be discussed as developments occur.
