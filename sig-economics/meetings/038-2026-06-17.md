### Revenue Sharing Proposal

* Tyler Wright introduced Isaac Grooms to present the **[Revenue Sharing Proposal](https://github.com/orgs/akash-network/discussions/1408)** currently under community discussion.
* Isaac Grooms explained that increasing utilization across provider infrastructure has created demand for a new revenue distribution framework.
* Reported that provider utilization historically averaged approximately 50% across participating clusters.
* Noted that utilization has increased significantly since March, particularly following the approval of PIP 3.5.
* Explained that customers are increasingly requesting long-term infrastructure reservations rather than relying solely on spot deployments.
* Stated that reservation-based deployments introduce additional operational complexity and financial risk for Overclock Labs.
* Explained that the proposal aims to fairly compensate Overclock Labs for:
  * Infrastructure management
  * Customer acquisition
  * Reservation management
  * Counterparty risk
  * Currency conversion costs
  * Price volatility exposure
  * Operational overhead
* Reviewed the historical funding model where nearly all provider earnings were returned to the Community Pool after expenses.
* Explained that the previous approach was intentionally generous toward the Community Pool but lacked clearly defined accounting rules.
* Introduced a simplified revenue-sharing model designed to improve transparency and consistency.

#### Proposed Revenue Distribution

* Spot deployment revenue:
  * 90% returned to the Community Pool.
  * 10% retained for operational expenses.
* Reserved deployment revenue:
  * 70% returned to the Community Pool.
  * 30% allocated to Overclock Labs.

### ACT Conversion & Accounting Process

* Isaac Grooms explained how Burn Mint Equilibrium changes provider accounting.
* Noted that every dollar spent on the network now contributes to AKT demand through token conversion.
* Explained that Overclock Labs has historically absorbed:
  * Conversion costs
  * Price volatility
  * Treasury exposure
* Discussed how provider earnings are accumulated as ACT before conversion.
* Cheng Wang clarified that ACT is non-transferable and therefore cannot be sent directly to the Community Pool.
* Explained that ACT must first be converted into AKT before any distributions can occur.
* Outlined the proposed accounting workflow:
  * Provider earnings converted from ACT into AKT daily.
  * Community Pool distributions performed weekly or monthly.
  * Quarterly reporting published summarizing Community Pool returns.
* Explained that denominating Community Pool distributions in AKT simplifies accounting and avoids unnecessary treasury volatility.

### Event Strategy

* Isaac Grooms explained that the proposal requests funding for:
  * Akash Accelerate
  * Hackathons
  * Regional ecosystem events
  * Developer outreach initiatives during the second half of 2026
* Explained that previous years relied heavily on Community Pool funding for ecosystem events.
* Stated that the team intentionally shifted strategy during 2026 toward:
  * Smaller events
  * More frequent engagement
  * Higher-quality developer acquisition
  * Better deployment outcomes
* Reported that hackathons have proven particularly successful in attracting developers likely to deploy applications on Akash.
* Explained that early results from the first half of 2026 have validated this strategy.
* Clarified that additional funding is intended to increase the number of successful outreach opportunities rather than increasing the size of individual events.

### Community Pool Funding Model for Events

* Isaac Grooms announced that Akash Accelerate will become a ticketed event for the first time.
* Confirmed that ticket revenue will ultimately be returned to the Community Pool.
* Explained that sponsorship revenue will also offset event costs.
* Noted that because sponsorship commitments are still being finalized, the proposal requests the full event budget upfront.
* Explained that after final accounting:
  * Ticket revenue
  * Sponsorship income
  * Remaining balances
  * Volatility buffers
  will all be reconciled before surplus funds are returned to the Community Pool.
