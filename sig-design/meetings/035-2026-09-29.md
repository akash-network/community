## Meeting Notes

### Akash Console UX & Onboarding Improvements

- Joshua shared updates on recent Akash Console improvements, including a redesigned onboarding experience and deployment configuration page.
- Early monitoring indicates that **new-user onboarding activation has increased approximately 5x** following the changes.
- Deployment creation time has also improved significantly, dropping from tens of minutes to seconds.
- These early results suggest that the simplified onboarding and deployment workflows are improving the user experience.
- The team will continue monitoring performance metrics and gathering feedback to validate the improvements over time.

### Deployment Management & Runtime Controls

- The Console deployments page has been redesigned with improved information architecture and both card and table views.
- A new deployment details page provides a clearer presentation of deployment information, particularly for complex deployments.
- The redesigned details experience received positive feedback during user testing.
- Users can now configure **deployment runtime limits**, specifying how long a deployment should run in hours or days.
- Runtime limits can also be modified after deployment creation, supporting workloads that require short bursts of compute rather than continuous operation.
- These changes aim to make deployment configuration and management more intuitive and flexible.

### Billing, Auto Top-Ups & Account Management

- Joshua highlighted improvements to billing and account balance management, with an emphasis on simplifying the escrow-related experience.
- The updated Console allows users to configure automatic balance top-ups for deployments that need to run continuously.
- Users can define either a fixed balance threshold or predictive spending criteria to manage automatic top-ups.
- Updated balance information is displayed to help users understand when the next automatic renewal or top-up is expected.
- The overall objective is to reduce manual balance management and create a more seamless deployment experience.

### Console Security & Upcoming Improvements

- The engineering team has implemented automatic spam-account detection to identify and flag accounts that appear to misuse Console.
- Joshua reported positive early results from the detection system.
- The team has also been collecting user feedback through analytics, testing, and design prototypes.
- A second iteration of the deployment configuration page is being prepared to further improve usability.
- The updated experience will be evaluated through additional monitoring and reporting.
- Tyler noted that further Console improvements are expected as the Core team prepares for an upcoming hackathon.

### Unified Design System & Brand Consistency

- Denis explained that the design team is working toward a **unified design system** across Akash products, the website, and other communication channels.
- The initiative focuses on using a consistent component library, visual assets, and design patterns across different Akash touchpoints.
- The objective is to ensure that Console, website pages, marketing materials, and other Akash experiences feel like parts of one connected ecosystem.
- Denis highlighted that recent website changes already reflect this direction, with components and design patterns being reused across products.

### Brand Assets & Community Resources

- A dedicated **Brand Assets** resource is available through the Akash website footer under Resources.
- Community members can access visual assets for social media posts, events, presentations, and other communication materials.
- These resources are intended to help contributors maintain consistent Akash branding when creating community content.
- Tyler encouraged builders and community members to reuse the available assets in products and projects built on Akash.

### Website Design & Content Updates

- Denis presented several website improvements completed with contributions from Joe Deng and other community members.
- New and updated pages include **Compare**, **Partner Program**, **Use Cases**, and **Enterprise**.
- The Enterprise page was developed with significant support from Joe and community contributors.
- The comparison pages help visitors evaluate Akash against alternative cloud providers.
- The Partner Program page introduces collaboration opportunities for organizations and community participants.
- The Use Cases section organizes website content around different audiences and applications.
- Additional visual improvements have been implemented across the website to create a more consistent look and feel.

### GPU Pricing & Provider Earnings Calculators

- The website pricing page has been improved to make it easier for users to estimate deployment costs.
- Popular deployment templates now provide quick pricing estimates, including GPU configurations.
- Users can also compare estimated Akash pricing with other cloud providers or customize compute configurations.
- A **Provider Earnings Calculator** has been introduced to help prospective providers estimate potential earnings.
- The calculator allows users to select GPU models and specify additional compute resources.
- Based on these inputs, users can view estimated monthly earnings denominated in AKT.
- These improvements are intended to make Akash pricing and provider economics easier to understand.

### Compute Providers Page Redesign

- Denis highlighted the redesigned Compute Providers page, which incorporates visual elements and design patterns from Console.
- The updated page presents network compute resources alongside an interactive globe displaying provider locations.
- Users can explore providers geographically, select individual providers, and review detailed information about their available resources.
- Provider profiles also display information about offerings and pricing.
- The redesign supports the broader goal of creating a consistent experience between the Akash website and Console.

### Provider Console Positioning

- Tyler reiterated that **Provider Console is now positioned as a provider management tool rather than a provider creation platform**.
- A banner has been added to Provider Console to communicate the change.
- Users interested in building an Akash provider are directed to **Provider Playbooks**.
- Future Provider Console improvements and related infrastructure discussions will continue through SIG Providers.

### Closing Remarks

- Tyler thanked Joshua and Denis for presenting the recent product and design improvements.
- Community members were encouraged to participate in SIG Design discussions and contribute to the open-source Akash website.
- Questions and design feedback can be shared asynchronously through the SIG Design channel.
- The next SIG Design meeting is expected in two months, with no regular meeting scheduled for October.
