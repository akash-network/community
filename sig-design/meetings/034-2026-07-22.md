## Meeting Notes

### Website & Design Updates

* Dennis welcomed attendees and provided an overview of recent design activities across the Akash ecosystem.
* The website continues to receive regular improvements through the Website Working Group, with ongoing development tracked publicly on GitHub.
* Joe Deng is leading several new website initiatives that will be shared with the community as they progress.
* Early planning is also underway for updates to the Akash ML website, the Home Node website, and several new website pages currently in the wireframing stage.

### Akash Accelerate

* A significant portion of the design team's effort is now focused on planning **Akash Accelerate**.
* Work is underway to finalize the event location, dates, venue, speakers, and branding.
* The Design SIG will support all creative assets, including the event website, social media graphics, presentations, venue branding, signage, merchandise, and promotional materials.

### Console Product Updates

* Josh shared that the redesigned Console onboarding experience was launched the previous week following multiple rounds of design reviews and user testing.
* Users can now sign in using email and a passcode, simplifying account access.
* The onboarding flow has been streamlined to reduce friction for new users.
* A new **Bid Pre-Check** feature now displays the number of potential providers while users configure their deployment, helping them avoid configurations that are unlikely to receive bids.
* The deployment workflow has been simplified into a more seamless create, confirm, and deploy experience.

### User Feedback & Future Plans

* Early user feedback and product metrics have been positive since the onboarding redesign launched.
* Based on the initial results, the team plans to adopt a similar onboarding experience for Akash ML after additional validation.
* Dennis noted that more product improvements are expected over the coming months and praised the updated user experience.
* Community members were encouraged to provide feedback through Discord or the SIG Design channel.
