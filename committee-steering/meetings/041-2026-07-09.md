## Meeting Notes

### Opening Remarks

* Tyler Wright welcomed attendees and explained that the meeting served as a catch-up session after the regular Steering Committee meeting was postponed.
* The objective was to summarize updates from recent SIGs and Working Groups rather than hold governance discussions.
* Members were invited to raise community concerns, agenda items, or feedback before the next full Steering Committee meeting.

### Community & Ecosystem Updates

* Tyler shared highlights from recent community events, including the AI World's Engineering Fair, where Akash ML and Akash Network were showcased to AI developers.
* Preparations were underway for an Akash-sponsored multi-agent hackathon in San Francisco, where attendees would experience the redesigned Console onboarding flow, Console Air, and new provider capabilities.
* The community continues to focus on increasing GPU supply across the network due to strong demand for H100, H200, and future B200 GPUs.
* Members were encouraged to connect potential data center operators and GPU providers with the Akash team through the Affiliates Program.
* Applications remain open for the third Student Ambassador Program cohort beginning in August.
* Tyler confirmed that **Akash Accelerate 2026** will take place on **October 5, 2026**, in San Francisco with speakers, workshops, breakout sessions, and community activities.
* Joe Deng encouraged community members interested in creating educational content and short-form videos to get involved in upcoming community initiatives.

### Product & Engineering Updates

* Tyler summarized ongoing engineering work across the Console, Provider, and Core Engineering teams.
* The redesigned Console onboarding experience is entering its first public release, introducing a significantly improved deployment workflow.
* A new **Bid Pre-Screening** feature allows users to view provider availability while configuring deployments, reducing failed deployment attempts.
* Console and Console Air continue to evolve as separate products with dedicated onboarding experiences.
* Upcoming provider releases will introduce **InfiniBand networking**, **Trusted Execution Environment (TEE)** support through Kata Containers, and full **ARM64 provider support**.
* Most upcoming product improvements will not require a network upgrade, while a smaller upgrade related to the Burn-Mint Equilibrium pricing oracle will be discussed separately in SIG Chain.
* Tyler noted that future roadmap discussions will include virtual machine and bare-metal support for the Akash Network.

### Community Feedback & Early Testing

* Kenny raised concerns about the prioritization of **Aep66**, explaining that Cloudflare remains a dependency when attaching custom domains, limiting fully decentralized deployment demonstrations.
* Tyler agreed to follow up with the Core Engineering team regarding the roadmap and priority of AP66.
* Community members were invited to participate in early testing of new Console features before public release.
* Joe announced an upcoming early user experience testing session for the redesigned Console deployment workflow and encouraged interested contributors to participate.

### Governance & Community Discussions

* Tyler reminded members that major governance discussions should begin within GitHub Discussions before progressing to SIGs and eventually the Steering Committee.
* Community feedback remains essential for determining feature priorities, grant proposals, budgets, and roadmap decisions.
* Tyler encouraged contributors to openly discuss ideas, feature requests, and implementation feedback within the public GitHub discussions.
* Updates were shared regarding transparent reporting for the PIP Spot Reservations revenue-sharing initiative, with reporting expected through a dedicated community repository.

### Closing Remarks

* Tyler previewed a much larger agenda for the next Steering Committee meeting scheduled for July 30.
* Community members were encouraged to submit agenda items early due to the number of planned discussions.
* Members were reminded to attend upcoming SIGs and Working Groups, monitor Discord for product announcements, and participate in early testing opportunities.
* The meeting concluded with appreciation for both the Core Engineering team and community contributors supporting ongoing development.
