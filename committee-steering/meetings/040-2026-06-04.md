## Meeting Notes

### Opening Remarks

* Tyler Wright welcomed participants and explained that the meeting was moved by one week due to scheduling conflicts.
* Community members were reminded that SIG, Working Group, and Steering Committee notes, recordings, project boards, and roadmaps are publicly available.
* No major community grievances or conflicts were raised.

### Akash Mainnet 18 Upgrade

* The Mainnet 18 proposal was on-chain, with the upgrade scheduled for June 11, 2026, subject to governance approval.
* The upgrade included BME Oracle improvements, Resource Reclamation, and a fix for the Market Order Close issue.
* Exchanges, major customers, and validators were being notified, with validator instructions expected before the upgrade.

### Provider Verification

* AEP 86 outlines a redesigned Provider Verification system that will replace the largely manual audit process.
* Phase One was being tested in a closed environment, with plans for a public testnet involving community providers.
* The new model will introduce ongoing and tiered verification rather than relying only on a one-time audit.
* Providers were encouraged to review the AEP and contribute through SIG Providers.

### Akash Console and Console Air

* Akash Console and Console Air have been separated into two distinct products.
* Console is becoming an enterprise-focused managed wallet experience, while Console Air remains the self-custodied crypto wallet option.
* The Core Team is redesigning Console onboarding and abstracting blockchain complexity for business users.
* Maxime Beauchamp also highlighted work on agent-focused deployments and an improved Akash Skill for tools such as Claude Code and Codex.

### Provider and Marketplace Development

* Confidential Compute development is progressing, including work involving Kata Containers and Trusted Execution Environments.
* InfiniBand support is being developed to improve support for advanced AI and GPU workloads.
* Marketplace V2 is being specified to introduce Virtual Machines and Bare Metal services.
* Several provider releases are expected, and providers were encouraged to maintain the correct Discord role for updates.

### Website, Documentation, and Student Ambassadors

* The Website and Documentation Working Group continues to improve the homepage, technical documentation, and community pages.
* Applications are open for the Fall 2026 Student Ambassador cohort beginning in August.
* Student Ambassadors contribute through documentation, application development, campus education, and hackathons.
* The program remains open to students globally.

### Akash Agents and Scribe

* Joe Deng confirmed that the current Akash Agents platform is operational using a bring-your-own-keys model.
* A new managed version is being developed, with Sandeep Narahari contributing to the platform.
* Akash Agents and Akash Scribe beta programs were expected to begin during June.
* Community members were encouraged to participate and provide feedback.

### Events and Ecosystem Growth

* Akash will sponsor a multi-agent hackathon scheduled for July 17, 2026.
* Additional event funding discussions are focused on attracting developers, providers, and enterprise users.
* The broader objective is to increase network usage and re-engage previous Akash users.

### Governance and Community Participation

* Community members were encouraged to provide public feedback on GitHub Discussions before proposals move on-chain.
* An updated proposal was available to recover AKT funds stuck in a bridge(umee).
* Tyler Wright emphasized that discussions should first take place within the relevant SIG or Working Group.
* Builders, providers, AKT holders, and non-technical contributors were encouraged to participate through Discord, GitHub, Telegram, and community programs.

### Closing Remarks

* Tyler Wright thanked participants and encouraged continued involvement in Akash’s open-source ecosystem.
* Cheng Wang also thanked attendees for their participation.
* The meeting concluded after approximately thirty minutes.
